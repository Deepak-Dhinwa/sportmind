# 2019-20 NBA Finals: Los Angeles Lakers vs Miami Heat

**Season:** 2019-20  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Los Angeles Lakers won 4-2

## Series Story

The Finals were played in the NBA Bubble in Orlando with no fans due to COVID-19. The Lakers won 4-2, with LeBron James averaging 29.8 PPG, 11.8 APG, and 8.5 RPG to win his fourth Finals MVP. Anthony Davis was dominant inside. Jimmy Butler had an extraordinary series for Miami (35.9 PPG in Game 3) but the Heat couldn't sustain it. The victory was dedicated to the memory of Kobe Bryant.
