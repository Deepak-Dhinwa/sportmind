# 2013-14 NBA Finals: Miami Heat vs San Antonio Spurs

**Season:** 2013-14  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** San Antonio Spurs won 4-1

## Series Story

The San Antonio Spurs avenged their 2013 Finals loss to the Miami Heat with a masterclass in team basketball. Kawhi Leonard won Finals MVP in just his third season. The Spurs' beautiful ball movement was epitomized by their 25-consecutive-pass scoring play in Game 4. LeBron James and the Heat had no answer.
