# 2022-23 NBA Finals: Denver Nuggets vs Miami Heat

**Season:** 2022-23  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Denver Nuggets won 4-1

## Series Story

The Denver Nuggets won their first championship in franchise history, defeating the Miami Heat 4-1. Nikola Jokic won Finals MVP with a historic triple-double series performance (30.2 PPG, 14.0 RPG, 7.2 APG). Jamal Murray averaged 26 PPG. The Heat made the Finals as an 8-seed — the first 8-seed to reach the Finals since 1999 — but were outmatched by Denver's depth and Jokic's dominance.
