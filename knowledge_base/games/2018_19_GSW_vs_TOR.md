# 2018-19 NBA Finals: Golden State Warriors vs Toronto Raptors

**Season:** 2018-19  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Toronto Raptors won 4-2

## Series Story

The Toronto Raptors won their first NBA championship, defeating the Warriors 4-2. Golden State lost Kevin Durant to an Achilles injury in Game 5 and Klay Thompson to an ACL injury in Game 6. Kawhi Leonard averaged 28.5 PPG and won Finals MVP. The Raptors were the first non-American team to win an NBA title. The Warriors dynasty effectively ended in this series.
