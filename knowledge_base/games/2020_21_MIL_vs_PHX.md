# 2020-21 NBA Finals: Milwaukee Bucks vs Phoenix Suns

**Season:** 2020-21  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Milwaukee Bucks won 4-2

## Series Story

The Milwaukee Bucks defeated the Phoenix Suns 4-2. Giannis Antetokounmpo was superhuman — his 50-point, 14-rebound, 5-block Game 6 clincher stands as one of the greatest individual Finals performances ever. Devin Booker averaged 27 PPG for Phoenix. The Bucks' victory ended a 50-year championship drought and validated Giannis's loyalty to the franchise.
