# 2017-18 NBA Finals: Cleveland Cavaliers vs Golden State Warriors

**Season:** 2017-18  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Golden State Warriors won 4-0

## Series Story

The fourth consecutive Warriors-Cavaliers Finals matchup — the first time the same two teams had met in four straight Finals since the Celtics and Lakers rivalry of the 1960s. The Warriors swept 4-0. LeBron averaged 34 PPG in the sweep but had limited support. JR Smith's inexplicable dribble-out in Game 1 overtime remains one of the most discussed moments in NBA Finals history.
