# 2014-15 NBA Finals: Cleveland Cavaliers vs Golden State Warriors

**Season:** 2014-15  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Golden State Warriors won 4-2

## Series Story

The Golden State Warriors won their first championship in 40 years. Andre Iguodala won Finals MVP, becoming the first Finals MVP since 1969 to not start every game. The Cavaliers were severely hampered by injuries to Kyrie Irving (broken kneecap) and Kevin Love (dislocated shoulder). LeBron James was heroic but undermanned.
