# 2021-22 NBA Finals: Boston Celtics vs Golden State Warriors

**Season:** 2021-22  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Golden State Warriors won 4-2

## Series Story

The Golden State Warriors won their fourth title in eight years, defeating the Boston Celtics 4-2. Stephen Curry finally won his first Finals MVP after three previous championships. Curry averaged 31.2 PPG in the Finals. The Warriors overcame a 2-3 series deficit with back-to-back road wins. Andrew Wiggins emerged as a two-way hero in this series.
