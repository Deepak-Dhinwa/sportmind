# 2016-17 NBA Finals: Cleveland Cavaliers vs Golden State Warriors

**Season:** 2016-17  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Golden State Warriors won 4-1

## Series Story

Kevin Durant's first championship with Golden State, silencing critics about his Finals performance under pressure. Durant averaged 35.2 PPG and won Finals MVP. The Warriors won 4-1, with Durant's dominant performances in Games 3, 4, and 5 being essentially unanswerable. LeBron James again averaged a triple-double but the talent gap was simply too large.
