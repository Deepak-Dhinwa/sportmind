# 2015-16 NBA Finals: Cleveland Cavaliers vs Golden State Warriors

**Season:** 2015-16  
**Type:** NBA Finals  
**Result:** Cleveland Cavaliers won 4-3

## Series Story

The most dramatic Finals in modern NBA history. The Cleveland Cavaliers became the first team to overcome a 3-1 series deficit in Finals history. LeBron James produced the greatest individual Finals performance ever — 29.7 PPG, 11.3 RPG, 8.9 APG. His chase-down block on Andre Iguodala and Kyrie Irving's three-pointer with 53 seconds left in Game 7 are the defining moments. The Warriors had won a record 73 regular-season games but could not close out the series.
