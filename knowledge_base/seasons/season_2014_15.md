# NBA Season 2014-15

**Champion:** Golden State Warriors  
**Season:** 2014-15

## Season Storyline

The Golden State Warriors won their first NBA championship in 40 years, defeating the Cleveland Cavaliers 4-2 in the Finals. Stephen Curry won his first MVP award as he led a revolution in three-point shooting. Andre Iguodala won Finals MVP after guarding LeBron James throughout the series. Coach Steve Kerr's first season resulted in a championship, with the Warriors' pace-and-space offense dazzling the league. The Cavaliers were hampered by injuries to Kevin Love and Kyrie Irving.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Stephen Curry (Golden State Warriors)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Kawhi Leonard (San Antonio Spurs)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Andrew Wiggins (Minnesota Timberwolves)
- **Finals MVP:** Andre Iguodala (Golden State Warriors)
- **Most Improved Player:** Jimmy Butler (Chicago Bulls)
