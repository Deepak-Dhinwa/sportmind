# NBA Season 2016-17

**Champion:** Golden State Warriors  
**Season:** 2016-17

## Season Storyline

Kevin Durant made the most controversial free agency decision in NBA history, joining the Golden State Warriors after losing a 3-1 series lead to them the previous year. The resulting superteam was virtually unstoppable, sweeping the Cleveland Cavaliers 4-1 in the Finals. Durant won Finals MVP. Russell Westbrook, spurned by his former co-star, responded by averaging a historic 31.6 PPG, 10.7 APG, and 10.4 RPG — the first player to average a triple-double for an entire season since Oscar Robertson.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Russell Westbrook (Oklahoma City Thunder)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Draymond Green (Golden State Warriors)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Malcolm Brogdon (Milwaukee Bucks)
- **Finals MVP:** Kevin Durant (Golden State Warriors)
- **Most Improved Player:** Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks)
