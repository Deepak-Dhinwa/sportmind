# NBA Season 2022-23

**Champion:** Denver Nuggets  
**Season:** 2022-23

## Season Storyline

The Denver Nuggets won their first NBA championship in franchise history, defeating the Miami Heat 4-1 in the Finals. Nikola Jokic, playing in his prime as a three-time MVP, won Finals MVP with a historic triple-double performance across the series. Jamal Murray's dynamic scoring and playoff heroics complemented Jokic perfectly. The Nuggets were the clear best team in the NBA all season and delivered on that promise in the postseason.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Joel Embiid (Philadelphia 76ers)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Evan Mobley (Cleveland Cavaliers)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Paolo Banchero (Orlando Magic)
- **Finals MVP:** Nikola Jokic (Denver Nuggets)
- **Most Improved Player:** Lauri Markkanen (Utah Jazz)
