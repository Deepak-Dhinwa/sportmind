# NBA Season 2019-20

**Champion:** Los Angeles Lakers  
**Season:** 2019-20

## Season Storyline

The NBA season was suspended in March 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic and resumed in the NBA Bubble in Orlando starting in July. The Los Angeles Lakers, led by LeBron James and Anthony Davis, won the championship, defeating the Miami Heat 4-2. The season carried extra emotional weight following the tragic death of Kobe Bryant and his daughter Gianna in a helicopter crash in January 2020. LeBron won his fourth title and his fourth Finals MVP with a third different team.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Ja Morant (Memphis Grizzlies)
- **Finals MVP:** LeBron James (Los Angeles Lakers)
- **Most Improved Player:** Brandon Ingram (New Orleans Pelicans)
