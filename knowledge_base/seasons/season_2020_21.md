# NBA Season 2020-21

**Champion:** Milwaukee Bucks  
**Season:** 2020-21

## Season Storyline

The Milwaukee Bucks won their first NBA championship since 1971, defeating the Phoenix Suns 4-2 in the Finals. Giannis Antetokounmpo delivered one of the greatest Finals performances in history, dropping 50 points, 14 rebounds, and 5 blocks in the clinching Game 6. The Greek Freak silenced questions about his ability to perform in crunch time and whether Milwaukee could win a title. Nikola Jokic won his first MVP award with the Denver Nuggets.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Nikola Jokic (Denver Nuggets)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Rudy Gobert (Utah Jazz)
- **Rookie of the Year:** LaMelo Ball (Charlotte Hornets)
- **Finals MVP:** Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks)
- **Most Improved Player:** Julius Randle (New York Knicks)
