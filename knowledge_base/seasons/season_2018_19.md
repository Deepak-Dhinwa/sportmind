# NBA Season 2018-19

**Champion:** Toronto Raptors  
**Season:** 2018-19

## Season Storyline

The Golden State Warriors faced a depleted roster in the Finals, losing Kevin Durant to injury before the series and Klay Thompson during Game 6. The Toronto Raptors, powered by Kawhi Leonard's transcendent performance, won their first-ever NBA championship. Leonard averaged 28.5 PPG in the Finals and won Finals MVP. His iconic buzzer-beater against Philadelphia in the second round had bounced on the rim four times before dropping. The Warriors' dynasty era effectively ended with this loss.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Rudy Gobert (Utah Jazz)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Luka Doncic (Dallas Mavericks)
- **Finals MVP:** Kawhi Leonard (Toronto Raptors)
- **Most Improved Player:** Pascal Siakam (Toronto Raptors)
