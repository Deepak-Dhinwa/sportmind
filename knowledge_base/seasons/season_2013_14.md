# NBA Season 2013-14

**Champion:** San Antonio Spurs  
**Season:** 2013-14

## Season Storyline

The San Antonio Spurs won their fifth NBA championship in dominant fashion, defeating LeBron James's Miami Heat 4-1 in the Finals. The Spurs played arguably the most beautiful team basketball in NBA history, sharing the ball and moving without the ball in a way that left the Heat helpless. Kevin Durant of Oklahoma City won the MVP award in one of the most emotional acceptance speeches in NBA history. The Heat, seeking their third consecutive title, were simply outclassed by a veteran Spurs squad hungry for revenge after the 2013 Finals heartbreak.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Kevin Durant (Oklahoma City Thunder)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Joakim Noah (Chicago Bulls)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Michael Carter-Williams (Philadelphia 76ers)
- **Finals MVP:** Kawhi Leonard (San Antonio Spurs)
- **Most Improved Player:** Goran Dragic (Phoenix Suns)
