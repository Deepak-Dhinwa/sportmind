# NBA Season 2017-18

**Champion:** Golden State Warriors  
**Season:** 2017-18

## Season Storyline

The NBA Finals featured a fourth consecutive Warriors-Cavaliers matchup — an unprecedented occurrence in NBA history. The Warriors swept the Cavaliers 4-0, with Kevin Durant again winning Finals MVP. LeBron James had an extraordinary individual performance, averaging 34 PPG in the Finals, but had little help. After the sweep, LeBron James left Cleveland for the Los Angeles Lakers, ending the Warriors-Cavaliers rivalry era.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** James Harden (Houston Rockets)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Rudy Gobert (Utah Jazz)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Ben Simmons (Philadelphia 76ers)
- **Finals MVP:** Kevin Durant (Golden State Warriors)
- **Most Improved Player:** Victor Oladipo (Indiana Pacers)
