# NBA Season 2021-22

**Champion:** Golden State Warriors  
**Season:** 2021-22

## Season Storyline

The Golden State Warriors completed a dynasty comeback, winning their fourth championship in 8 years by defeating the Boston Celtics 4-2. Stephen Curry finally won his first Finals MVP award after three previous championships. The Warriors did it with a core of Curry, Klay Thompson, and Draymond Green supplemented by young players Jordan Poole and Andrew Wiggins. Nikola Jokic won his second consecutive MVP award.

## Award Winners

- **Most Valuable Player:** Nikola Jokic (Denver Nuggets)
- **Defensive Player of the Year:** Marcus Smart (Boston Celtics)
- **Rookie of the Year:** Scottie Barnes (Toronto Raptors)
- **Finals MVP:** Stephen Curry (Golden State Warriors)
- **Most Improved Player:** Dejounte Murray (San Antonio Spurs)
