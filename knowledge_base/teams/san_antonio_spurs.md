# San Antonio Spurs

**Abbreviation:** SAS  
**City:** San Antonio  
**Conference:** West

**Championships (2013–2023):** 2013-14

## Team Overview

The Spurs capped a dynasty under Gregg Popovich with the 2014 championship, defeating LeBron James's Miami Heat with beautiful team basketball. Tim Duncan, Tony Parker, and Manu Ginobili's final title run showcased textbook ball movement. They also developed Kawhi Leonard into a two-way star who would eventually lead Toronto to the 2019 title. The Spurs' sustained excellence across decades is unmatched in modern NBA history.

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **LaMarcus Aldridge**, **Patty Mills**, **Kawhi Leonard**, **DeMar DeRozan**, **Tony Parker**
