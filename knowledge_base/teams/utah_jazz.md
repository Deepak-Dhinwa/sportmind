## Team Overview

The Utah Jazz compete in the Western Conference, Northwest Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 43 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2020-21 (52-20, 0.722 win%). Their most difficult season was 2013-14 (25-57).
