The New Orleans Pelicans compete in the Western Conference, Southwest Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 36 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2017-18 (48-34, 0.585 win%). Their most difficult season was 2015-16 (30-52).
