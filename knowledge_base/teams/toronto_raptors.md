**City:** Toronto

**Championships (2013–2023):** 2018-19

## Team Overview

The Raptors won their first and only NBA championship in 2018-19, powered by Kawhi Leonard's iconic 'Kawhi Shot' buzzer-beater in the second round against Philadelphia and his dominant Finals performance against an injury-depleted Warriors squad. Leonard's 60-point, four-bounce buzzer-beater in the second round became one of the most memorable moments in NBA playoff history. Pascal Siakam developed into an All-Star alongside him.

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **Kyle Lowry**, **DeMar DeRozan**, **Pascal Siakam**, **Fred VanVleet**, **Jonas Valančiūnas**
