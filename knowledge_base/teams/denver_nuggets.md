## Team Overview

The Nuggets won their first NBA championship in 2022-23, led by Nikola Jokic's unprecedented three-time MVP dominance. Jokic's unique combination of scoring, passing, and rebounding at center redefined what the position could be. Jamal Murray's scoring punch complemented Jokic perfectly, and their chemistry was unmatched in the league. The Nuggets defeated the Miami Heat 4-1 in the Finals, with Jokic winning his first Finals MVP award.

## Key Players (2013–2023)
