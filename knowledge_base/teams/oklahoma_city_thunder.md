# Oklahoma City Thunder

**Abbreviation:** OKC  
**City:** Oklahoma City  
**Conference:** West  
**Division:** Northwest  

## Team Overview

The Thunder were a consistent Western Conference contender led by Kevin Durant and Russell Westbrook during the early part of the decade. Durant's 2014 MVP season, highlighted by his emotional 'You are the real MVP' speech to his mother, remains one of the most moving moments in NBA history. After Durant's departure to Golden State in 2016, Westbrook won the MVP with a historic 42-triple-double season in 2016-17.

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **Russell Westbrook**, **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander**, **Kevin Durant**, **Steven Adams**, **Paul George**
