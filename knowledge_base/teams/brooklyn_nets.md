## Team Overview

The Brooklyn Nets compete in the Eastern Conference, Atlantic Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 36 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2020-21 (48-24, 0.667 win%). Their most difficult season was 2016-17 (20-62).
