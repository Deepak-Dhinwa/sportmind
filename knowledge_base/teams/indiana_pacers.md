## Team Overview

The Indiana Pacers compete in the Eastern Conference, Central Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 42 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2013-14 (56-26, 0.683 win%). Their most difficult season was 2021-22 (25-57).
