## Team Overview

The Minnesota Timberwolves compete in the Western Conference, Northwest Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 33 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2017-18 (47-35, 0.573 win%). Their most difficult season was 2014-15 (16-66).

## Key Players (2013–2023)
