## Team Overview

The Los Angeles Clippers compete in the Western Conference, Pacific Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 49 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2013-14 (57-25, 0.695 win%). Their most difficult season was 2017-18 (42-40).

## Key Players (2013–2023)
