# Golden State Warriors

**Abbreviation:** GSW  
**City:** San Francisco  
**Conference:** West  
**Division:** Pacific  
**Championships (2013–2023):** 2014-15, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2021-22

## Team Overview

The Warriors were the defining dynasty of the 2010s, winning four NBA championships in eight years (2015, 2017, 2018, 2022). Built around 'Splash Brothers' Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson under coach Steve Kerr, they revolutionized basketball with their three-point shooting and ball movement. Kevin Durant joined in 2016-17, forming one of the most talented rosters in NBA history. Their 73-9 regular season record in 2015-16 remains the best single-season record in NBA history. Stephen Curry became the first unanimous MVP in 2015-16.

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **Stephen Curry**, **Klay Thompson**, **Draymond Green**, **Kevin Durant**, **Andrew Wiggins**
