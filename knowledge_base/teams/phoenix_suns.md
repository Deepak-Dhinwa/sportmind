## Team Overview

The Phoenix Suns compete in the Western Conference, Pacific Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 37 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2021-22 (64-18, 0.780 win%). Their most difficult season was 2018-19 (19-63).
