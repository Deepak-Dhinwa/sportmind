# Chicago Bulls

**Abbreviation:** CHI  
**City:** Chicago  
**Conference:** East  
**Division:** Central  

## Team Overview

The Bulls were competitive in the early 2010s, led by Derrick Rose who won the MVP in 2010-11 as the youngest MVP in NBA history. Injuries derailed their championship window. Jimmy Butler emerged as a star during this era before eventually being traded. The team went through a difficult rebuild during the latter part of the decade.

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **Zach LaVine**, **Jimmy Butler III**, **Nikola Vučević**, **DeMar DeRozan**, **Lauri Markkanen**
