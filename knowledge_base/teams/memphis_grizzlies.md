## Team Overview

The Memphis Grizzlies compete in the Western Conference, Southwest Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 42 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2021-22 (56-26, 0.683 win%). Their most difficult season was 2017-18 (22-60).
