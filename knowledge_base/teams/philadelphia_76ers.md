## Team Overview

The Philadelphia 76ers compete in the Eastern Conference, Atlantic Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 38 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2020-21 (49-23, 0.681 win%). Their most difficult season was 2015-16 (10-72).
