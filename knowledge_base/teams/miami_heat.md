# Miami Heat

**Abbreviation:** MIA  
**City:** Miami  
**Conference:** East  
**Division:** Southeast  

## Team Overview

The Heat's Big Three era (LeBron James, Dwyane Wade, Chris Bosh) ended with their 2014 Finals loss to San Antonio. LeBron then departed for Cleveland. Miami subsequently rebuilt and made a surprising run to the 2020 NBA Finals in the Orlando Bubble, showcasing Jimmy Butler's elite leadership and toughness. The Heat's culture emphasizes conditioning, hard work, and defensive intensity known as 'Heat Culture.'

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **Goran Dragic**, **Bam Adebayo**, **Dwyane Wade**, **Jimmy Butler III**, **Hassan Whiteside**
