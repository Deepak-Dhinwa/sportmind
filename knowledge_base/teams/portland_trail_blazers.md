## Team Overview

The Portland Trail Blazers compete in the Western Conference, Northwest Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 43 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2013-14 (54-28, 0.659 win%). Their most difficult season was 2021-22 (27-55).
