# Los Angeles Lakers

**Abbreviation:** LAL  
**City:** Los Angeles  
**Conference:** West  
**Division:** Pacific  
**Championships (2013–2023):** 2019-20

## Team Overview

The Lakers ended a decade-long championship drought by winning the 2020 NBA title in the COVID Bubble in Orlando. LeBron James and Anthony Davis formed a dominant duo. LeBron became the first player to win Finals MVP with three different franchises. The franchise legend Kobe Bryant passed away in January 2020, adding emotional weight to the championship run as the team dedicated their title to his memory.

## Key Players (2013–2023)

Top contributors by scoring volume: **LeBron James**, **Anthony Davis**, **Kyle Kuzma**, **D'Angelo Russell**, **Kentavious Caldwell-Pope**
