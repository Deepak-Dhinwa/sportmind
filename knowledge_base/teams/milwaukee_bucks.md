## Team Overview

Giannis Antetokounmpo developed from a raw, skinny 18-year-old into a two-time MVP and NBA champion. The Bucks won the 2021 championship with Giannis delivering an extraordinary 50-point performance in the clinching Game 6 against Phoenix, finishing with 14 rebounds and 5 blocks. It was the Bucks' first title since 1971 and validated Giannis's decision to remain in Milwaukee despite being pursued by bigger-market teams.

## Key Players (2013–2023)
