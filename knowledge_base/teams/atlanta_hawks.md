## Team Overview

The Atlanta Hawks compete in the Eastern Conference, Southeast Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 39 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2014-15 (60-22, 0.732 win%). Their most difficult season was 2017-18 (24-58).
