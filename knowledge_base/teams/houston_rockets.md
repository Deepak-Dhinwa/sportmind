## Team Overview

The Houston Rockets compete in the Western Conference, Southwest Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 43 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2017-18 (65-17, 0.793 win%). Their most difficult season was 2020-21 (17-55).

## Key Players (2013–2023)
