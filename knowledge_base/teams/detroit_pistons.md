## Team Overview

The Detroit Pistons compete in the Eastern Conference, Central Division. Over the 2013-2023 decade, they averaged 30 wins per season. Their best season in this era was 2015-16 (44-38, 0.537 win%). Their most difficult season was 2022-23 (17-65).
