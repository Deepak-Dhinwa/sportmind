# Cleveland Cavaliers

**Abbreviation:** CLE  
**City:** Cleveland  
**Conference:** East  
**Division:** Central  
**Championships (2013–2023):** 2015-16

## Team Overview

The Cavaliers returned LeBron James to Cleveland in 2014 after his stint with Miami. They reached the Finals four consecutive years (2015-2018). Their 2016 championship stands as one of the greatest upsets in sports history — they became the first team to overcome a 3-1 Finals deficit, defeating the record-setting 73-win Warriors. LeBron James's chase-down block on Iguodala and Kyrie Irving's clutch three-pointer in Game 7 are iconic moments in NBA history.
