Built app

SportMind

Ten NBA seasons live in a graph, a document store, and a few small models. You ask in English.

Neo4jRAGReact
  1. 01 · Ingest2013-14 through 2022-23: players, teams, games, awards, into Neo4j and a vector store.
  2. 02 · AskChat streams from the API. Stats, awards, and head-to-head are direct graph queries.
  3. 03 · PredictA win model from box scores, plus a per-player forecast for points, assists, and rebounds.
  4. 04 · ShowThe graph draws the neighborhood. Your own notes can be uploaded into the same store.

Question

  • What did this player do, how is this team connected, and what is a fair win chance from the box score alone?

Why this path

  • A table can rank scorers. A graph can walk from a player to a team, a ring, and an award in one picture.
  • Win probability is a separate model because the prose store should not be inventing a percentage.

What held

10seasons, 2013-14 to 2022-23
~1,400players in the graph
6,246document chunks for chat

Reading the result

  • The win model holds out 2022-23 and lands at AUC 0.63, accuracy 61%. That is the usual band for box scores with no in-game tracking.
  • Player forecasts are 658 short-memory models, 94 players by 7 stats. On the same holdout, points miss by about 5 and assists by about 1.6.
  • Thirty-two win features were trimmed to form, rest, head-to-head, and venue. Probabilities are calibrated.

What you can click

  • Scorers, a player page, champions, awards, a comparison, and a head-to-head.
  • Neighborhood graph for a player or team, and a league view of all 30 teams.
  • A markdown note can be indexed and then asked about in chat.

Next pass. Box scores have a ceiling. Richer tracking data is what would move the win model.

Stack

Neo4j, Weaviate, FastAPI, React. Chat over a websocket. Win model is XGBoost. Forecasts are per-player EWMA.

Look at it

Docker Desktop open, then docker start neo4j weaviate, then .\run.ps1. The script does not start the databases. App at http://localhost:5173.
